# AI Assistance
We used OpenAI Codex (logged model identifier: gpt-6.1-sol) for discussions of organizational visibility and topology, comparisons of role-specific information in ROMA and TalkHier to inform the intervention, discussions of trajectory-analysis methods and existing benchmark metrics, and language and structural revision of author-provided material. The authors retain responsibility for the study design, evidence, interpretations, and final manuscript. Representative retained interactions and a brief description of their purposes are provided in ai_assistance/representative_interactions.json. These are selected records of multi-turn assistance, not a complete conversation log. Exact prompts and model-version information for interactions beyond these selected records have not been established in this disclosure; their purposes are summarized from the authors' report, without reconstructing prompts or inferring earlier model versions. The experimental agents and collaboration judge use qwen3.7-flash, separately documented in the execution configurations.
